`[HEADLINE]`
# Five ways to put AI to work in your pub

`[BYLINE SENTENCE, bold]`
Here, Peter Pitcher MBII sets out what has changed, five ways to start and the shortcuts that make it quicker:

`[SECTION 1]`
## 1. See your pub the way AI does
Nearly one in five British consumers already use AI tools such as ChatGPT to plan a pub or restaurant visit, checking reviews, prices, menus and opening times (Zonal and NIQ, October 2026). Since April 2026, Google's AI Mode has linked UK searchers straight to booking partners. Ask an assistant where to go for Sunday lunch in your town and check what it says about you. Google's own advice is the basics: important content, such as menus and hours, as text on your website, and an up-to-date Business Profile.

`[SECTION 2]`
## 2. Tell it who you are, once
ChatGPT keeps standing instructions that apply to every chat: Settings, then Personalization, or Customize ChatGPT on a phone. Give it your pub's name and town, who eats and drinks with you, and how you write. Add one line: never invent prices, dates, opening times or allergen information.

`[SECTION 3]`
## 3. Show it, don't type it
Tap the + icon and choose Add photos & files. Photograph the specials board, a delivery note or a supplier's price list and ask for a tidy table. Or tap the microphone and talk it through. Leave out anything with a guest's or a team member's name on it.

`[SECTION 4]`
## 4. Bring your own idea
We have run tasting nights at The Anchor for years. Now AI helps us turn each idea into a full plan, handle messages with suppliers, design the artwork and build an app that runs the night, with guests rating each drink on their phones. The idea is still ours: AI makes it happen faster.

`[SECTION 5]`
## 5. Keep three rules
First, guests' details, staff records and allergy information stay out of free and personal accounts: the data protection regulator says there is no AI exemption. In Settings, Data controls, switch off "Improve the model for everyone". Second, a named person checks every date, price and claim, because these tools can state wrong things as fact. Third, AI never decides what is in a dish and never writes a review: allergen accuracy is your legal duty, and an invented review is a fake review in law.

`[BOX: tinted panel, can be lifted out]`
## Shortcuts worth knowing
- **/** In ChatGPT, signed in, type / and choose Search to make it check the live web.
- **@** Calls in an app you have connected. In Gemini, type @ and pick one, such as Google Calendar.
- **###** Put ### or triple quotation marks before and after text you paste in, so the assistant can tell your instruction from your material.
- **#** Not a command in any of the main assistants: it marks a heading in a long request.
- **Not shortcuts** The "secret codes" shared online, such as /ELI5, are not features. They work only as ordinary words.
- Checked October 2026. Menus move.
