# Three ways to put AI to work in your pub

`[BYLINE SENTENCE, bold]`
Here, Peter Pitcher MBII sets out three ways to start and how far it can go:

`[SECTION 1]`
## 1. Start with a conversation
Nearly one in five British consumers use AI tools to plan a pub or restaurant visit (Zonal and NIQ, October 2026). So open ChatGPT, tap the Voice icon and talk to it about your pub. Then tell it who you are, once: Settings, then Personalisation. Add one rule: never invent prices, dates or allergen information.

`[SECTION 2]`
## 2. Make it a business partner
At The Anchor the ideas and the personality stay ours. AI helped us build our website in our own voice, develop menu ideas, plan our tasting nights and build one back-office system that shows the whole business together.

`[SECTION 3]`
## 3. Keep the human jobs human
Complaints and sensitive messages go to a person. Guests' details, staff records and allergy information stay out of free and personal accounts. AI never decides what is in a dish, and never writes a review: an invented review is a fake review in law.
