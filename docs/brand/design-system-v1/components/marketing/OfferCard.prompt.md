Engagement card without prices — pricing and offer naming are open decisions, so it sells scope and outcome instead.

```jsx
<OfferCard featured eyebrow="Start here" name="Growth Diagnostic"
  blurb="Two weeks inside your business. What is blocking growth, what it costs you, what to do."
  includes={['Leadership and data review','Pressure-point map','Prioritised build plan']}
  footnote="Fixed fee, agreed on the scoping call."
  cta={{label:'Book a diagnostic',href:'/diagnostic'}} />
```

`featured` = ink card for the one recommended entry point. When pricing is decided, add it above the footnote; do not invent numbers.
