Peter Pitcher, founder. Also the licensee of The Anchor.

Orange Jelly started with a business we could not walk away from: our own.

Peter spent years building growth systems inside companies by day and running The Anchor, a village pub, by night. The pub became the proving ground. Every method, every piece of practical AI, every pricing decision was tested on a real P&L where a bad idea costs real money the same week.

That habit became the company. We get under the skin of a business, expose what is actually blocking growth and build the fix: creativity, technology and commercial judgement, with AI where it earns its place.

We challenge the problem, never the person. Experienced operators do not need a lecture. They need a partner who says the uncomfortable thing, then builds the way out.
