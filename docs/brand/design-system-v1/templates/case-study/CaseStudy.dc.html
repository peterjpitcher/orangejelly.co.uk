403% more table bookings. No extra ad spend.

The Anchor · Growth Partnership · 12 months · Verified against an agreed baseline

This story The problem What we heard What we built What changed

A wet-led village pub with quiet midweek sessions, a booking journey that lost customers and no way to be found by the people already searching. Twelve months later the same rooms, the same team and a transformed P&L.

The problem

Revenue depended on a handful of busy sessions. Bookings arrived by phone or not at all, no-shows went unmanaged and the pub was invisible for every search that mattered. The obvious answer, spend on social, had been tried. It filled feeds, not tables.

What we heard

HEAR before EXPOSE: the team was not short of effort, it was short of a system. Customers loved the place once they found it. Finding it, and booking it, was the leak.

What we built

A bookable journey with confirmations and reminders, structured local search presence, event formats designed for the quiet sessions and a weekly measurement rhythm. Practical AI where it removed drag, nowhere else.

What changed

Demand moved from luck to system. Bookings quadrupled without extra ad spend, no-shows collapsed and search became the biggest source of new customers.
