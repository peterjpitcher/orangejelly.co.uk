A £2 discount on a £15 dish feels harmless. Run the numbers and it can demand 40% more volume just to stand still.

The real cost of a discount

Discounts come straight off the margin, not the price. At a 30% food margin, £2 off a £15 dish gives away 44% of the profit in that sale, and the extra covers rarely arrive.

A content calendar will not fix a broken growth system.

Worked example

  • Dish price £15, margin £4.50
  • With £2 off, margin falls to £2.50
  • Volume needed to stand still: +80% on that dish

Quick test: if the promotion still looks good with the uplift halved, run it. If not, it is a margin leak.

Raise perceived value instead: reprice the anchor items, fix the journey, measure the change.
