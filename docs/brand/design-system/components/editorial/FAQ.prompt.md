Accordion for the FAQ block most pages carry. Ruled rows; + becomes × when open; multiple rows can stay open.

```jsx
<FAQ openFirst items={[
  {q:'How fast do we see results?',a:'Quick wins inside 30 days. Structural change is measured over a quarter, with the baseline agreed up front.'},
  {q:'Do you replace our marketing team?',a:'No. We fix the growth system they work inside.'}
]} />
```

Answers accept nodes for multi-paragraph copy. Say the important thing in the first sentence; mirror the same Q/A pairs in FAQPage schema.
