# Approved positioning refinement: 5 September 2026

Orange Jelly builds websites, bespoke applications and connected customer systems, using AI where it adds value, to drive business growth. This is the current public offer and takes precedence over the earlier exploratory positioning below.

Lead with what a customer can buy. Websites connect discovery to enquiries and bookings. Applications connect customer records, portals and everyday workflows. Useful AI performs a defined task with suitable human checks. Explain "digital backbone" through these examples.

The audience remains small and mid-sized businesses across sectors. Hospitality provides strong evidence through The Anchor, our own venue, without defining the entire business. A customer with a clear website or application project is welcome.

Discovery establishes the right scope, existing-system fit and measures. It does not obscure the build offer. AI used in development does not make an ordinary website an AI-powered product. No new delivery deadline, guarantee, package, support commitment or price is introduced.

Public headline: **Websites and connected systems that grow your business.**

## Historical positioning record

The original discussion follows for context. Its facts remain subject to CLAIMS.md and current company records; its broad consultancy-first messaging is superseded by the approved refinement above.

Orange Jelly
Business, Brand & Positioning Overview
1. The simplest definition
Orange Jelly is a strategic growth partner for small businesses.
We work with ambitious business owners who want to take control of growth.
Our role is not simply to sell them marketing, technology, consultancy or another predefined service. We understand what they are trying to achieve, identify what is genuinely standing in the way, build the right strategy and then develop and implement the solutions required to move the business forward.
Marketing will often form part of the answer because it is one of Orange Jelly's strongest capabilities. But it is a solution, not the solution.
The fundamental principle is:
Understand what is really happening before prescribing what should happen next.
There must be a clear line of sight between the business challenge, the strategy and the solution.
2. The emerging positioning
The strongest positioning territory from the discussion is:
For business owners ready to take control of growth.
And beneath that:
Orange Jelly is a strategic growth partner for ambitious businesses.
That language matters.
Orange Jelly is not primarily targeting people who want rescuing, reassuring or hand-holding.
The ideal customer believes there is more potential in their business and is prepared to act to realise it.
They are proactive.
They are ambitious.
They are prepared to make changes.
They want more from the business they have built.
The emotion we initially explored was relief, but that turned out not to be quite right. The stronger territory is control, ambition, achievement and growth.
3. The customer mindset
The right Orange Jelly customer is thinking something closer to:
My business could be bigger than this.
Or:
I know there is more potential here, but I need the right partner to work out how to unlock it.
They may want:

* more profit
* more customers
* a stronger business
* better marketing
* more control
* greater commercial confidence
* a business that operates better
* personal financial security
* greater choice over how they live
* the satisfaction of building something significant
* the achievement that comes from seeing their business reach its potential

The precise personal motivation will vary.
For one owner it may eventually mean financial freedom.
For another it may be building a much larger organisation.
For another it may be recognition or achievement.
For another it may be the ability to step away from the business occasionally without everything falling apart.
That is why growth is the common language.
And control is particularly important.
The owner should increasingly feel that they are directing the trajectory of the business rather than merely reacting to whatever happens to it.
4. Bigger is the aspiration. Better is the route.
One useful distinction from the conversation was:
Bigger is the dream. Better is the method.
Orange Jelly is interested in business owners who want to build something bigger.
But simply increasing revenue is not automatically growth.
A bigger business with terrible margins, broken systems, poor customer experiences or an unclear strategy may simply become a bigger problem.
Orange Jelly therefore works on making the business better in order to make it bigger.
Growth needs to be commercially sensible, controlled and sustainable.
5. Why Orange Jelly exists
Orange Jelly grew from the intersection of two core strengths:
creativity and technology.
The original instinct behind the business was to create somewhere that could make full use of creativity, communication, technology, process design, strategic thinking and problem solving together.
But the purpose has evolved beyond simply combining those capabilities.
At its heart, Orange Jelly exists because businesses are frequently sold solutions before anyone has properly established what their problem actually is.
A business says:
"We need more marketing."
An agency sells marketing.
But perhaps the real issue is:

* customers come once and never return
* the experience is poor
* margins are too low
* staff do not care
* the owner is disconnected from the operation
* money is leaking unnecessarily
* the product is wrong
* processes are broken
* nobody understands the strategy
* the proposition is unclear

Generating more customers may simply pour more people into a broken experience.
That does not solve the business problem.
Orange Jelly exists to challenge that.
6. The core philosophy
Diagnose before prescribing.
If a company has strong margins but not enough customers, that is very likely a marketing and growth challenge.
Orange Jelly can then investigate questions such as:

* How do we attract more people?
* How do we reach new audiences?
* How do we increase customer frequency?
* How do we encourage customers to spend more?
* How do we improve retention?
* How do we create loyalty?
* How can marketing work harder?

But if the company is generating significant revenue and none of it is becoming profit, adding more revenue may not solve anything.
The work may instead begin with margins, expenditure, processes or commercial strategy.
That distinction is central to Orange Jelly.
The answer follows the evidence.
Not the service catalogue.
7. Orange Jelly does not take the stated problem at face value
One of the strongest characteristics of the approach is inquisitiveness.
A customer may describe the symptom rather than the cause.
For example:
"We have digital darts but nobody books them. We need marketing."
Marketing may ultimately be part of the solution.
But Orange Jelly would first investigate the experience.
Perhaps:

* the darts are hidden in a dark corner
* the area is dirty
* the equipment does not work properly
* staff do not understand it
* staff do not promote it
* the booking journey is difficult
* customers do not understand the proposition

In that situation, launching an advertising campaign first would be irresponsible.
The problem has to be unpicked from the ground upwards.
8. Business strategy sits underneath everything
A major development from our conversation was recognising that strategy has to sit above the individual solutions.
Orange Jelly should not become a collection of clever tactical fixes.
The work needs to connect back to the bigger business.
Many small businesses operate with very little genuine business strategy.
Their understanding of performance may amount to:
"What did we take this week?"
But strategy asks much bigger questions:
What are we building?
Who is it for?
Why should customers choose it?
Where should the company grow?
What needs to change?
What type of business do we want to become?
What should we stop doing?
What capabilities are missing?
What does success look like?
How should the business operate?
What needs to be true for growth to happen?
Orange Jelly therefore works in the territory of underlying business strategy, not simply marketing strategy.
Marketing, technology, AI, customer experience, systems and process then become levers used to deliver that strategy.
9. Growth with confidence
There is also an important philosophy around the type of growth Orange Jelly believes in.
Growth should not happen so quickly or carelessly that the business becomes a house of cards.

* training them properly
* creating strong ways of working
* choosing the right partners
* making good commercial decisions
* putting foundations underneath growth

The ambition is not growth at any cost.
It is confident, sustainable growth.

22. Orange Jelly does not pretend to be every specialist

So Orange Jelly can own the problem-solving journey without claiming to personally possess every specialist capability.

"Success"
True but generic unless defined.
"Problem"
Essential to the methodology but potentially overused in customer-facing copy.

"Empire"
