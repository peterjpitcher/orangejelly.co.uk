# 02. Positioning and Messaging

## Primary category

**Growth partner for ambitious small and mid-sized businesses.**

This is the clearest external category because it communicates both the outcome and the working relationship.

For search, procurement or explanatory contexts, Orange Jelly may also use:

**Technology-enabled business growth consultancy.**

The two phrases perform different jobs:

- `Growth partner` is human, active and commercially attractive.
- `Business growth consultancy` helps people understand and find the category.

Neither should be replaced with `AI consultancy` or `marketing agency` as the master description.

## Positioning statement

For ambitious small and mid-sized businesses that have built something real but reached a ceiling, Orange Jelly is a hands-on growth partner that exposes what is blocking progress and builds practical solutions across demand, conversion, margin, operations and customer experience.

Unlike agencies that sell a fixed service, Orange Jelly starts with the business problem and uses the right combination of creativity, technology, AI and commercial thinking to make measurable change happen.

## The shortest useful explanation

> **We help ambitious businesses solve growth problems.**

## The core proposition

> **You bring the growth problem. We build the solution.**

## The supporting proposition

> Orange Jelly gets under the skin of your business, exposes the pressure points and builds practical solutions using creativity, technology and AI that earns its place.

## The elevator pitch

Orange Jelly helps ambitious small and mid-sized businesses break through growth barriers. We work directly with owners and leaders to understand what is really happening, identify the pressure points and build the fix. That could involve marketing, conversion, margin, operations, systems, automation or AI. We do not arrive with a pre-selected product. We start with the problem, agree what success looks like and make change happen.

## The growth pressure points

Orange Jelly should describe capabilities through the problems they solve rather than a long agency service list.

### 1. Create demand

Reach the right people, sharpen the proposition and build a stronger route into the business.

Possible work includes brand, marketing strategy, campaigns, content, acquisition, local visibility and partnerships.

### 2. Convert more

Improve the journey from interest to enquiry, booking, purchase, repeat purchase or higher value.

Possible work includes websites, booking journeys, sales processes, CRM, customer communications, pricing presentation and funnel optimisation.

### 3. Protect margin

Find where value is leaking and improve the commercial model.

Possible work includes pricing, product mix, procurement, supplier negotiations, labour deployment, utilities, waste and operational choices.

### 4. Remove operational drag

Replace unnecessary friction, manual work and fragmented processes with clearer systems.

Possible work includes workflow redesign, automation, internal tools, stock and inventory processes, reporting, SOPs and team operating rhythms.

### 5. Improve the experience

Create a better experience for customers and the people delivering it.

Possible work includes service design, customer research, communications, digital touchpoints, training, feedback loops and experience standards.

### 6. Build for scale

Create the systems, information and capabilities needed for the next stage of growth.

Possible work includes dashboards, data foundations, repeatable playbooks, technology architecture, AI-enabled workflows and scalable operating models.

## What Orange Jelly is

- A growth partner
- A commercial problem solver
- A challenger with the ability to build
- A bridge between creativity and technology
- A source of practical AI advantage
- A hands-on partner to owners and leadership teams
- Outcome-led and evidence-minded

## What Orange Jelly is not

- A day-to-day social media posting service
- A cheap source of marketing tasks
- A generic digital agency
- An AI consultancy selling technology for its own sake
- A business coach offering encouragement without implementation
- A slide-deck consultancy that diagnoses and disappears
- A hospitality-only business
- A partner for leaders who want business as usual

## Key differentiators

### Problem first

Orange Jelly does not ask which service the client wants. It asks what is blocking growth and how the client will know it has changed.

### Connected thinking

Marketing, margin, operations and customer experience are treated as parts of one growth system rather than separate departmental problems.

### Creativity plus technology

Orange Jelly combines imaginative commercial thinking with the ability to design practical systems, workflows, digital experiences and AI-enabled tools.

### Built in the real world

The approach has been shaped inside live trading businesses, not only in presentations or theoretical models.

### Challenge plus delivery

Orange Jelly is willing to say the difficult thing, but it also helps build and embed the solution.

### Small enough to move

Orange Jelly can work directly with decision makers, move quickly and adapt without the drag of a large consultancy.

## Lines to own

- **You bring the growth problem. We build the solution.**
- **AI is part of the toolkit, not the product.**
- **Built for businesses big enough to have real growth problems and small enough to move fast.**
- **A content calendar will not fix a broken growth system.**
- **We find where growth is being blocked and make the right change happen.**
- **Brilliant thinking is useless until it changes something.**
- **We are not here to preserve business as usual.**

## Language to avoid as the lead

- AI support for small businesses
- Hospitality marketing agency
- Full-service digital agency
- Social media management
- Marketing solutions for every business
- Cutting-edge AI transformation
- Helping businesses unlock their potential

These phrases are either too narrow, too generic or too likely to attract the wrong work.

## Recommended one-line bio

**Orange Jelly is the growth partner for ambitious small and mid-sized businesses, combining creative problem solving, technology and practical AI to make measurable change happen.**
