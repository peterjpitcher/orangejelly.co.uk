# 10. Website Blueprint

## The website's job

The new website must do four things quickly:

1. Explain that Orange Jelly is a growth partner, not a hospitality marketing agency.
2. Help the right visitor recognise their growth problem.
3. Prove that Orange Jelly can diagnose and build, not only advise.
4. Make the next step feel clear, serious and worth taking.

## Primary visitor action

**Book a Growth Diagnostic**

A stronger conversational label may be used in prominent locations:

**Bring us the problem**

The action can lead to a qualification form before calendar access.

## Recommended navigation

- Growth problems
- How we work
- Results
- Insights
- About
- Bring us the problem

Avoid leading the navigation with a long `Services` menu.

## Recommended site structure

### Home

The high-level proposition, problem recognition, method, pressure points, proof, fit and primary call to action.

### Growth problems

A hub and individual pages for:

- Growth has stalled
- Demand or pipeline is weak
- Leads, enquiries or bookings are not converting
- Margin is under pressure
- Operations are slowing the business
- Customer experience is leaking value
- The business wants to use AI or automation intelligently
- Systems cannot support the next stage of scale

### How we work

- HEAR. EXPOSE. BUILD. PROVE.
- What access is required
- How success is agreed
- What a Growth Diagnostic includes
- How Orange Jelly moves from diagnosis to implementation

### Solutions or capabilities

This page may exist for clarity and search, but it should be organised around outcomes:

- Create demand
- Convert more
- Protect margin
- Remove operational drag
- Improve experience
- Build for scale

Each section can show the types of marketing, technology, commercial and operational work that may be used.

### Results

- The Anchor as a live business laboratory
- Full case studies
- Short proof cards by pressure point
- Verified metrics
- Client quotes
- Examples of outputs and systems

### Insights

Problem-led content organised by the search territories in `09-search-vision.md`.

Hospitality should become a visible specialist collection within Insights, not the main definition of the company.

### About

- Why Orange Jelly exists
- Peter's intersection of creativity, marketing, technology and AI
- The real-business testing philosophy
- The team and partner model
- Values and way of working

### Start here or Growth Diagnostic

- Who it is for
- What happens
- What the client receives
- What Orange Jelly needs
- Typical duration
- Investment guidance once decided
- Qualification form

### Contact

A simple fallback page. It should not be the main conversion route.

## Homepage wireframe and draft direction

### 1. Hero

**Eyebrow:** Growth partner for ambitious small and mid-sized businesses

**Headline:** Growth stuck? Bring us the problem.

**Supporting copy:** Orange Jelly gets under the skin of your business, exposes what is holding you back and builds practical solutions using creativity, technology and AI that earns its place.

**Primary action:** Book a growth diagnostic

**Secondary action:** See how we work

### 2. Recognition

Headline:

**You do not need more activity. You need to know what will move the numbers.**

Show common symptoms:

- Growth has stalled.
- Leads are not converting.
- Margin is disappearing.
- Manual work is slowing the team down.
- The business has outgrown its systems.
- Everyone has a different view of the problem.

### 3. The promise

**You bring the growth problem. We build the solution.**

Explain that Orange Jelly may use marketing, commercial change, process, technology, automation or AI according to what the business needs.

### 4. Growth pressure points

Six concise cards:

- Create demand
- Convert more
- Protect margin
- Remove operational drag
- Improve experience
- Build for scale

### 5. Method

**HEAR. EXPOSE. BUILD. PROVE.**

Use one sentence for each step and link to the full method page.

### 6. Proof

Lead with one strong Anchor story framed as a live small-business case study. Follow with proof cards across bookings, revenue, margin, operations, systems and customer experience once verified.

### 7. Fit

Headline:

**Big enough to have real growth problems. Small enough to move fast.**

Describe the client who will get the most value and the behaviours that are a poor fit.

### 8. Engagement options

- Growth Diagnostic
- Growth Sprint
- Growth Partnership
- Transformation Programme

Keep the explanation outcome-led and avoid overwhelming detail.

### 9. Final action

Headline:

**Stop circling the problem.**

Supporting line:

Tell us what is happening, what you have tried and what needs to change.

Action:

**Bring us the problem**

A stronger founder-led campaign version may add `Make the f***ing change`, but the main website should earn that level of intensity before using it.

## Enquiry form

Recommended fields:

- Name
- Work email
- Company
- Website
- Role
- Approximate employee or revenue band
- What is happening in the business?
- What do you believe is blocking growth?
- What would success look like?
- Why now?
- Who is involved in the decision?
- Indicative investment range
- Preferred next step

Avoid asking the visitor to choose `social media`, `website`, `AI` or another delivery service before diagnosis.

## Conversion principles

- Keep one dominant call to action.
- Repeat it at natural decision points.
- Use proof close to claims.
- Explain what happens after enquiry.
- Show who is and is not a fit.
- Provide enough investment context to discourage unsuitable leads.
- Use simple forms with conditional follow-up where needed.
- Make direct owner and managing director access feel like a feature.
- Show actual outputs and business changes, not only polished brand imagery.

## Content migration priorities

### Keep and reposition

- Strong hospitality insights with proven search demand
- The Anchor results and learnings
- Useful guides that address transferable business problems
- Founder credibility around marketing, technology and AI

### Rewrite

- Homepage and category description
- About page
- Contact and calls to action
- Capability pages
- Case studies
- Any page that leads with hospitality marketing

### Redirect or retire

- Outdated pricing or hourly-rate pages
- Pages that contradict current website or technology capabilities
- Thin articles with no search or commercial value
- Duplicate pages competing for the same intent
- Pages that make Orange Jelly appear to be a generic social media service

## Design direction

The website should feel:

- Bold but easy to navigate
- Distinctive without becoming experimental at the expense of conversion
- Human and commercially credible
- Fast, accessible and mobile-first
- Rich in proof, diagrams and examples
- Free from generic AI imagery

Suggested interaction ideas:

- A visual Growth Pressure Map
- A problem selector that reveals connected causes
- Before-and-after case-study journeys
- Small working demos of tools or automations
- Animated pressure or movement devices used with restraint

## Measurement plan

Track:

- Qualified enquiry conversion by page
- Growth Diagnostic starts and completions
- Visitor movement from insight to problem page to proof
- Non-hospitality versus hospitality discovery
- Engagement with method and case studies
- Problem categories generating enquiries
- Lead quality, decision-maker seniority and budget fit
- Proposal conversion
- Revenue influenced by the website

## Website test

A suitable visitor should understand within 20 seconds:

- Orange Jelly helps businesses grow.
- It starts with the problem rather than a service.
- It can work across commercial, operational and technical issues.
- AI is useful but not forced.
- The work is for ambitious businesses ready to change.
- There is a clear next step.
