# Orange Jelly Brand and Growth System

**Version 0.1 | Working foundation | 25 August 2026**

This pack turns the founder discovery conversation into a practical source of truth for reshaping Orange Jelly. It is designed for three uses:

1. To keep strategic decisions clear while the business evolves.
2. To brief designers, copywriters, developers and delivery partners.
3. To give Codex, Claude Code or another AI build tool enough context to create the website and supporting assets without pulling Orange Jelly back towards its old hospitality marketing position.

## The central decision

Orange Jelly is no longer defined as a hospitality marketing agency.

Orange Jelly is the **growth partner for ambitious small and mid-sized businesses**.

It gets under the skin of a business, exposes what is blocking growth, and builds practical solutions using the right combination of creativity, technology, AI and commercial thinking.

The product is not AI. The product is not marketing. The product is **brilliant problem solving that creates measurable business change**.

## Core proposition

> **You bring the growth problem. We build the solution.**

Supporting explanation:

> Orange Jelly helps ambitious small and mid-sized businesses break through growth barriers. We expose the pressure points and build practical solutions across demand, conversion, margin, operations and customer experience. AI is part of the toolkit, not the product.

When two documents appear to conflict, use this order:

1. Company vision and positioning
2. Ideal client and brand identity
3. Tone of voice and signature method
4. Offer architecture and proof framework
5. Search and website documents
6. Activation plan

`13-open-decisions.md` records matters that are not yet final. Do not quietly make those decisions during design or development.

## Decisions already made

- Growth is the organising idea.
- Orange Jelly is broader than hospitality.
- Hospitality remains valuable proof, not the category definition.
- The target is ambitious small and mid-sized businesses, not the whole small-business market.
- Orange Jelly should normally work with owners, managing directors or leaders who have authority to act.
- Orange Jelly starts with a business problem and an agreed definition of success.
- Marketing, technology, systems and AI are tools, not pre-selected products.
- Orange Jelly is not a day-to-day social media posting service.
- Orange Jelly should challenge clients and make change happen, not preserve business as usual.
- Impact must be visible and measurable.
- AI should be used when it creates a genuine advantage, not because it is fashionable.

## Working language

These lines are approved as the current strategic foundation:

- **Growth partner for ambitious small and mid-sized businesses.**
- **You bring the growth problem. We build the solution.**
- **AI is part of the toolkit, not the product.**
- **Built for businesses big enough to have real growth problems and small enough to move fast.**
- **We are not here to be liked. We are here to make change happen.**

The last line is a manifesto statement, not necessarily the homepage headline.
