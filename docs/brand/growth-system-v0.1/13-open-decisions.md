# 13. Open Decisions

This document separates confirmed strategy from choices that still need approval. It prevents designers, developers or AI tools from making permanent decisions by accident.

## Decisions required before website copy is final

### 1. Final category pairing

**Recommended default:**

- Headline category: `Growth partner for ambitious small and mid-sized businesses`
- Supporting/search category: `Technology-enabled business growth consultancy`

Decision: Confirm whether `consultancy` should appear visibly on the homepage or mainly in metadata and explanatory copy.

### 2. Final core line

**Recommended default:**

`You bring the growth problem. We build the solution.`

Decision: Confirm this as the permanent brand promise rather than a launch campaign line.

### 3. Signature method name

**Recommended default:**

`HEAR. EXPOSE. BUILD. PROVE.`

Decision: Test whether `EXPOSE` feels constructively challenging or too aggressive in client conversations.

### 4. Primary call to action

**Recommended default:**

- Button: `Book a growth diagnostic`
- Campaign or section line: `Bring us the problem`

Decision: Confirm whether the first step is always a paid diagnostic or whether selected enquiries can enter through a free fit call.

### 5. Target business size

**Recommended default:**

`Typically 10 to 500 employees, with fit based on complexity, budget, authority and ambition rather than headcount alone.`

Decision: Choose whether this range should be public, used only for qualification, or replaced with revenue bands.

### 6. Minimum commercial threshold

Decision required:

- Minimum diagnostic fee
- Minimum sprint value
- Minimum monthly partnership value
- Minimum programme value

This is one of the most important filters against low-value work.

### 7. Sector strategy

**Recommended default:**

Remain sector-open at the top level. Present hospitality as a proof-rich specialist area. Add other sector pages only after credible evidence exists.

Decision: Identify two or three sectors where Peter's network, credibility or problem set creates the best near-term route to non-hospitality revenue.

### 8. Geographic scope

Decision: UK only, UK-first with international work, or fully international.

The website, pricing, legal terms and search strategy depend on this.

### 9. Founder versus company brand

**Recommended default:**

Lead with Orange Jelly as the company and use Peter prominently as founder, practitioner and point of view.

Decision: Determine how much delivery is expected to remain founder-led and how quickly a visible team or partner network needs to be established.

### 10. Delivery model

Decision required:

- What Orange Jelly builds directly
- What is delivered through specialist partners
- Whether partners are white-labelled, introduced or subcontracted
- Quality and commercial standards
- Ownership of client relationships

### 11. Swearing boundary

**Recommended default:**

Use partially censored expletives sparingly in founder-led social, manifesto content and selected campaign lines. Keep core service, proposal and formal content clean.

Decision: Confirm whether any expletive appears on the permanent homepage.

### 12. Visual identity scope

Decision: Evolution of the existing identity or a complete redesign.

Before deciding, review:

- Current logo equity
- Legibility
- Digital flexibility
- Whether the current look reinforces hospitality or agency associations
- Ability to support a more commercially authoritative brand

### 13. Proof approval

Decision required for every claimed result:

- Verified number
- Baseline
- Date range
- Data source
- Context
- Permission

No existing percentage should be copied into the new site until this is complete.

### 14. Legacy hospitality content

**Recommended default:**

Keep high-value content, move it into a hospitality insight hub, update the Orange Jelly description and strengthen commercial pathways.

Decision: Which pages remain strategically valuable and which should be redirected or removed.

### 15. Search priorities

Decision required after keyword research:

- First three problem clusters
- Priority industries
- Geographic modifiers
- Search tools or calculators to build
- Balance between high-intent commercial pages and broader authority content

## Decisions that can wait until after launch

- Final names for every offer
- A larger proprietary research programme
- Additional sector hubs
- A formal certification or partner programme
- A complex membership or subscription product
- A broad productised AI catalogue
- A complete library of operational templates

## Decision rule

When uncertain, choose the option that most clearly reinforces:

1. Growth as the outcome
2. Problem-first diagnosis
3. Practical build capability
4. Measurable impact
5. Ambitious clients ready to change

Do not choose based only on what is easiest to sell this week if it recreates the positioning problem Orange Jelly is trying to escape.
