# 12. 90-Day Activation Plan

## Objective

Move Orange Jelly from a confused hospitality marketing position to a credible, commercially usable growth-partner brand, with a website and sales system capable of attracting suitable work beyond hospitality.

## Phase 1: Decide and prepare, weeks 1 to 3

### Week 1: Approve the strategic core

Decide:

- Primary category and one-line description
- Core proposition
- Vision and mission
- Ideal client and disqualifiers
- Signature method
- Tone boundaries

Output:

- Approved versions of documents 01 to 06
- One-page internal brand summary

Exit criterion:

Peter can explain Orange Jelly consistently in 30 seconds, 2 minutes and 10 minutes.

### Week 2: Set the commercial model

Decide:

- Offer names
- Scope and outputs
- Minimum engagement values
- Delivery capacity
- Partner model
- Client responsibilities
- Qualification and proposal process

Output:

- Approved offer architecture
- Initial pricing framework
- Enquiry qualification form
- Proposal template brief

Exit criterion:

Every suitable enquiry has a clear starting route and every unsuitable enquiry can be declined without improvisation.

### Week 3: Audit proof and the existing site

Complete:

- Verify current performance claims
- Identify available case-study evidence
- Capture Anchor artefacts across all growth pressure points
- Audit all live pages, indexed pages and outdated offers
- Mark content to keep, rewrite, merge, redirect or remove

Output:

- Proof register
- Content inventory
- Redirect requirements
- List of evidence gaps

Exit criterion:

No unverified claim is treated as approved and no legacy page is allowed to define the future position by accident.

## Phase 2: Build the brand and commercial experience, weeks 4 to 8

### Week 4: Visual identity direction

Create:

- Moodboard and visual territories
- Colour and typography options
- Graphic system
- Photography direction
- Data and case-study design style
- Accessibility review

Output:

- Selected visual direction
- Initial design tokens
- Brand application examples

Exit criterion:

The identity feels serious about outcomes, recognisably Orange Jelly and clearly broader than hospitality.

### Week 5: Website content architecture

Create:

- Final sitemap
- Page goals
- Search intent by page
- Required proof by page
- Primary and secondary calls to action
- Content migration map

Output:

- Approved website blueprint
- Page-level content briefs

Exit criterion:

Every page has a job and every journey leads towards a suitable commercial action.

### Week 6: Core copy and proof

Write and approve:

- Homepage
- Growth problems hub
- How we work
- Growth Diagnostic
- About
- Results overview
- First Anchor case study
- Enquiry journey

Output:

- Approved core copy
- Proof assets and placeholders clearly labelled

Exit criterion:

A visitor can understand the new business without relying on future content.

### Week 7: Design and build

Implement:

- Responsive page components
- Growth Pressure Map
- Case-study modules
- Insight templates
- Qualification form
- Analytics events
- Technical SEO foundations

Output:

- Working website in staging

Exit criterion:

The site is fast, accessible, measurable and usable across mobile and desktop.

### Week 8: Migration and quality assurance

Complete:

- Content migration
- Redirects
- Metadata
- Structured data where appropriate
- Form testing
- Analytics validation
- Accessibility checks
- Proof and claim review
- Cross-browser checks

Output:

- Launch candidate

Exit criterion:

No old positioning, outdated pricing or broken journey remains visible without a deliberate reason.

## Phase 3: Launch and create demand, weeks 9 to 12

### Week 9: Controlled launch

- Launch the site
- Announce the new position to existing network and clients
- Brief referral partners
- Update social profiles, email signatures and company descriptions
- Monitor enquiries, search visibility and technical issues

Output:

- Consistent market-facing identity
- Initial feedback log

### Week 10: Problem-led content launch

Publish an initial set of high-value pages or articles across:

- Stalled growth
- Conversion
- Margin or operational drag
- Practical AI adoption

Each piece must have proof, a next action and a defined commercial job.

Output:

- First search cluster
- Internal linking and conversion paths

### Week 11: Direct business development

- Build a priority prospect list
- Develop a founder-led outreach narrative
- Contact suitable existing relationships
- Ask trusted contacts for introductions
- Offer the Growth Diagnostic as the clear starting point
- Capture objections and language used by prospects

Output:

- Active pipeline
- Refined qualification language

### Week 12: Review and sharpen

Review:

- Lead quality
- Reasons people engage or decline
- Search and content behaviour
- Conversion through the website
- Which pressure points create the strongest interest
- Whether the offer and price filter correctly
- Whether the new category is understood

Output:

- 30-day optimisation backlog
- Updated messaging and offer decisions
- Priority proof and content plan

## Measures for the 90 days

### Brand clarity

- Can target prospects explain Orange Jelly accurately after seeing the site?
- Has the proportion of people describing it as a social media or hospitality marketing agency reduced?

### Demand quality

- Number of qualified enquiries
- Seniority of contacts
- Business size and budget fit
- Mix of hospitality and non-hospitality opportunities
- Problem categories represented

### Commercial performance

- Diagnostics sold
- Proposal conversion
- Average engagement value
- Pipeline value
- Revenue from new positioning

### Search performance

- Non-hospitality impressions and visits
- Visibility for problem-led searches
- Movement from insights to proof and diagnostic pages
- Qualified enquiries influenced by organic search

## Operating rule

Do not delay the new position until every possible document, page and case study is perfect. Approve the strategic core, build a credible minimum version and improve it through evidence.

Do not launch while the old and new descriptions openly contradict each other.
