# 06. The Orange Jelly Method

## Working name

**HEAR. EXPOSE. BUILD. PROVE.**

This is the recommended version-one method. It uses active, strong language and reflects the way Orange Jelly wants to work. The name can be refined later, but the operating logic should remain.

## Why a method matters

Orange Jelly's range could otherwise look unfocused. A named method shows that different solutions are connected by a consistent way of working.

The method answers:

- How can Orange Jelly work across marketing, margin, operations and technology without becoming a generalist supplier?
- What does a client actually experience?
- How does Orange Jelly avoid producing activity without impact?
- How does the business know when to stop?

The answer is a repeatable discipline that starts with the problem and ends with evidence.

## 1. HEAR

**Understand what is really happening, not only what the brief says.**

Orange Jelly gets close to the operating reality. It hears from the leader, the people doing the work, the data and, where useful, the customer.

Typical activity:

- Owner or leadership interviews
- Staff and stakeholder conversations
- Customer feedback or observation
- Review of commercial and performance data
- Journey, process and systems walkthroughs
- Review of existing plans, tools, suppliers and activity
- Identification of fixed constraints and assumed constraints

Outputs may include:

- Initial problem statement
- Current-state map
- Evidence gaps
- Baseline measures
- Stakeholder and customer themes

The discipline:

> Listening is not enough. Orange Jelly must hear the difference between what people say is happening and what the business evidence shows.

## 2. EXPOSE

**Find the pressure points, root causes and opportunities that matter.**

Orange Jelly connects the visible symptoms to the wider growth system. It challenges comfortable explanations and makes trade-offs clear.

Typical activity:

- Root-cause analysis
- Growth-system mapping
- Funnel and conversion analysis
- Margin and cost analysis
- Process and workflow diagnosis
- Customer journey review
- Technology and data assessment
- Opportunity sizing
- Prioritisation by impact, confidence, effort and speed

Outputs may include:

- Growth Pressure Map
- Agreed problem definition
- Prioritised opportunities
- Constraints and dependencies
- Success measures
- Recommended intervention

The discipline:

> Do not solve the loudest symptom. Expose the few pressure points that can materially change the outcome.

## 3. BUILD

**Design and implement the right solution.**

The solution may be creative, commercial, operational or technical. It may combine several disciplines. AI is used when it improves the answer, not because it is part of the sales pitch.

Possible builds include:

- A new proposition or campaign
- A website or conversion journey
- A lead, booking or sales system
- A pricing or product-mix change
- A reporting dashboard
- An internal workflow or automation
- A stock, inventory or procurement process
- An AI-enabled tool or assistant
- A customer communication programme
- A team operating rhythm, SOP or decision framework
- A supplier or cost optimisation plan

Build principles:

- Start with the smallest intervention capable of creating material change.
- Prototype early when uncertainty is high.
- Design for the people who must use the solution.
- Make ownership and dependencies explicit.
- Build measurement into the solution.
- Avoid unnecessary technology.
- Document what the client needs to operate after handover.

## 4. PROVE

**Measure the difference, improve the solution and embed what works.**

Orange Jelly agrees success before building, then tracks whether the intervention changed the relevant commercial or operational outcome.

Typical activity:

- Compare against the baseline
- Review leading and lagging indicators
- Test and iterate
- Capture qualitative feedback
- Resolve adoption issues
- Document lessons and operating guidance
- Decide whether to scale, adjust or stop

Outputs may include:

- Impact report
- Before-and-after measures
- Learning log
- Handover and operating guidance
- Scale recommendation
- Case-study evidence, subject to client permission

The discipline:

> Work is not complete because a deliverable was supplied. It is complete when the agreed change is implemented, understood and measured to the extent the engagement allows.

## The required starting agreement

Before material work begins, Orange Jelly and the client should agree:

1. The problem being solved
2. Why it matters now
3. The baseline or current evidence
4. What success looks like
5. What is within scope
6. Who can make decisions
7. Who owns implementation
8. What data and access are required
9. How progress will be reviewed
10. What would cause the work to stop, change or expand

## The Growth Pressure Map

This should become a recognisable Orange Jelly diagnostic asset. It maps the business across six connected areas:

1. Demand
2. Conversion
3. Margin
4. Operations
5. Experience
6. Scale

For each area, assess:

- Current performance
- Evidence quality
- Primary pressure points
- Commercial consequence
- Opportunity size
- Dependencies
- Recommended next move

The map is not a generic scorecard. It should be tailored to the client's business model and used to focus action.

## The method in one line

> **Hear the business. Expose the pressure points. Build the fix. Prove the impact.**

## What the method prevents

- Starting with a requested tactic before understanding the problem
- Treating every issue as marketing
- Using AI where it adds no value
- Delivering a strategy the client cannot implement
- Measuring activity instead of impact
- Continuing work after the useful outcome has been achieved
- Allowing the client to avoid necessary decisions

## Working artefacts

The method should eventually have templates for:

- Discovery interview guide
- Data request
- Current-state map
- Growth Pressure Map
- Problem statement
- Success scorecard
- Opportunity prioritisation
- Build brief
- Experiment plan
- Implementation tracker
- Impact report
- Handover pack

These operational templates are a later build. The method in this document is the strategic source of truth.
