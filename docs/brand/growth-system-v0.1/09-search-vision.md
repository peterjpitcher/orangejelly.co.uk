# 09. Search Vision

## The search ambition

**Orange Jelly should become the result ambitious business leaders find when they know something is blocking growth but cannot yet name the right solution.**

The current content has often been useful enough for people to read and continue alone. The new search strategy must remain genuinely useful while creating a clearer route from information to diagnosis, trust and conversation.

The goal is not traffic for its own sake. The goal is to be visible at the moments when an owner or leader thinks:

- Something is not working.
- We have tried the obvious fixes.
- This problem is bigger than one channel or department.
- We need outside challenge and practical help.

## Search audience

Priority searchers include:

- Owner or founder
- Managing director
- Commercial director
- Operations director
- Marketing director
- Transformation or innovation lead

They may search using `small business`, `SME`, their sector or no company-size term at all. Orange Jelly should optimise for the problem language they use, not only its preferred category language.

## Search positioning

Orange Jelly should show up as:

- A credible interpreter of messy growth problems
- A practical alternative to a narrow agency or large consultancy
- A source of connected commercial thinking
- A guide to useful AI rather than AI theatre
- A partner that can move from diagnosis to implementation

## Core search territories

Detailed keyword research is still required. The strategic territories are:

Examples of intent:

- How to grow an established small business

Examples of intent:

## Intent ladder

### Symptom-aware

The searcher knows what they can see but not the cause.

Content job: Name connected causes, reduce confusion and offer a diagnostic next step.

### Problem-aware

The searcher has a hypothesis about the issue.

Content job: Help them validate the problem, understand trade-offs and see why a wider view may be required.

### Solution-aware

The searcher is comparing approaches such as an agency, consultant, software platform or internal hire.

Content job: Explain Orange Jelly's method, fit, commercial model and ability to build.

### Partner-aware

The searcher is evaluating Orange Jelly directly.

Content job: Provide proof, clarity, reassurance, qualification and a simple route to start.

## Content types

### Problem pages

Evergreen commercial pages built around real business problems, such as:

- Growth has stalled
- Leads are not converting
- Profit is disappearing
- Manual processes are holding us back
- We want to use AI but do not know where to start

These should be more prominent than service pages.

### Diagnostic articles

Explain why a symptom may have several causes and help the reader assess the situation.

### Decision guides

Help leaders choose between options, for example software versus process change, an internal hire versus a partner, or automation versus simplification.

### Tools and scorecards

Useful self-assessments, calculators and checklists that reveal the need for deeper diagnosis.

### Case studies

Show how a connected problem was solved and make the lesson transferable.

### Founder point of view

Strong, experience-led content that challenges fashionable or lazy answers.

### Sector hubs

Hospitality can remain an important hub. Additional sector hubs should only be added when Orange Jelly has real proof and repeatable insight.

## The commercial job of every article

No article should exist only to attract traffic.

Before creating content, define:

1. The exact problem or decision
2. The likely searcher
3. The stage of intent
4. The useful insight Orange Jelly can add
5. The relevant proof
6. The next action
7. The offer the content supports

## Recommended content structure

1. Name the symptom in the reader's language.
2. Explain why the obvious explanation may be incomplete.
3. Show the connected pressure points.
4. Give a useful diagnostic approach.
5. Explain the limits of a generic or do-it-yourself answer.
6. Provide relevant proof or an example.
7. Offer the right next step.

The content should not withhold useful information to force a call. It should make clear when the quality of diagnosis, implementation or cross-functional change determines the outcome.

## Conversion path

Recommended ladder:

1. Read an insight
2. Use a relevant scorecard, checklist or diagnostic prompt
3. See a connected case study
4. Understand the Orange Jelly method
5. Assess fit
6. Book or enquire about a Growth Diagnostic

## Calls to action

Avoid generic `Contact us` language where possible.

Use problem-led actions such as:

- Bring us the problem
- Book a growth diagnostic
- Tell us what is blocking growth
- Pressure-test your next move
- Find the pressure points
- See whether we are a fit

## Search and conversion requirements

- Every article should link to a relevant problem page.
- Every problem page should link to a relevant case study.
- Every case study should explain the next suitable offer.
- High-intent pages should show fit, method, investment expectations and decision-maker access.
- Forms should ask about the problem and desired outcome, not ask visitors to choose from a long service list.
- Analytics should track article-to-problem-page movement, case-study engagement, diagnostic starts and qualified enquiries.

## Legacy content principle

Useful hospitality content should not be deleted simply because the positioning has widened.

It should be:

- Audited for accuracy and conflicting positioning
- Contained within a clear hospitality insight hub
- Updated with the new Orange Jelly description
- Connected to broader growth principles
- Given stronger commercial pathways
- Redirected or retired where pricing, services or promises are outdated

## Search success measures

Traffic is only an early indicator. The useful measures are:

- Visibility for priority problem-led searches
- Growth in non-hospitality discovery
- Visits from target roles and suitable businesses
- Movement from insights to problem pages and case studies
- Diagnostic enquiries
- Qualified opportunities
- Revenue influenced by organic search
- Sectors and problem types generating the strongest fit

## Search vision in one line

> **Show up when a serious business leader realises the problem is bigger than another quick fix.**
