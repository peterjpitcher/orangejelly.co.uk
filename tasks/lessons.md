Patterns from Peter's corrections. Read at the start of a session.

## 22 September 2026: do not cap what respondents can say

**What happened:** the first survey's two app screens were built as "pick up to three". Peter
asked for "as many as they'd like".

**Pattern:** when the job is research, a limit on answers is a product decision that throws
away data, and it was not asked for. Default to letting people pick everything that applies,
and get the forced choice from a separate "if you could only have one" question instead.

**How to apply:** before adding a cap, minimum, required field or cut-off to anything a
customer answers, check it was asked for. If it seems useful, propose it rather than build it.
