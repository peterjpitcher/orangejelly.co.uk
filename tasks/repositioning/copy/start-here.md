## What actually happens

**Section heading:** what actually happens.

1. **You tell us what is happening.**
   Four questions on the form at the bottom of this page. A couple of sentences on each is plenty.

2. **We read it and reply.**
   A person reads it, not a filter, and the reply is about your situation rather than a brochure.

3. **We talk for an hour.**
   Free, and not a pitch. We ask about the numbers, the history and what has already been tried. Most of
   that hour is us listening, which is the first step of how we work for a reason.

4. **We tell you what we think.**
   Including when the answer is that you do not need us. That happens, and saying so is worth more to
   both of us than three weeks of finding out.

5. **If it is worth going further, we scope it.**
   A defined piece of work, a fixed fee agreed before anything starts, and a measure we both sign up to.

## What you get from the hour, whether or not we work together

**Section heading:** what you get from the hour.

- Our read on what is actually blocking growth, said plainly.
- The two or three things we would look at first, and why those.
- A straight answer on whether we are the right people for it.

You are not buying anything in that conversation and we are not asking you to.

## What we need from you

**Section heading:** what we need from you.

- **An hour with someone who can act on the answer.** Not a gatekeeper taking notes back to a committee.
- **The real numbers.** We cannot find a margin problem in a business that will only show us its revenue.
- **A willingness to be told something you disagree with.** Politely, with the reasoning, and only when we
  think it matters. But we will say it.

## Who this works for

**Section heading:** big enough to have real problems. small enough to move fast.

You will get the most from this if:

- You have built something real and it has stopped growing the way it used to.
- You are busy, and the numbers are not moving in proportion to the effort.
- The problem crosses more than one part of the business, and nobody quite agrees what it is.
- The systems you are running were built for a smaller company.
- You want someone to challenge the thinking, not agree with it.
- You can act once a direction is agreed.

Roughly, that tends to mean 10 to 500 people. It is a guide rather than a rule: a smaller business with
real scale, urgency and something to invest fits fine.

## Who this does not work for

**Section heading:** who this does not work for.

**Intro:** We would rather say this now than three weeks in. This is not a fit if:

- **You want someone to post three times a week.** Posting more will not fix a proposition, a conversion
  journey or an offer. If posting is genuinely the gap we will say so, but it is not what you would be
  hiring us for.
- **You have decided the plan and need a pair of hands.** We are not the cheapest way to execute a
  decision that is already made, and we would be bad value doing it.
- **You cannot give us access to the people, the numbers or the customers.** Diagnosis without access is
  guessing, and we will not charge for guessing.
- **You want AI because it is AI.** It is part of the toolkit. If the answer to your problem is a pricing
  change and a phone call, that is what you will get.
- **You want the decision validated.** If the direction is set and the question is really "tell us this is
  right", a second opinion you have paid for is worth nothing.
- **Nothing inside the business can actually change.** Growth that requires no change is not something
  anyone can sell you.

**Close:** None of that makes yours a bad business. It makes us the wrong supplier, which is a different
thing and a much cheaper one to find out early.

## How long, and what it costs

**Section heading:** how long, and what it costs.

**The conversation** is an hour, and it is free.

**A diagnostic** is normally two to three weeks. Longer work depends entirely on what we find, and we
agree the shape of it with you before it starts rather than after.

**On cost:** we do not publish prices, because we do not sell packages. What we would quote a company of
eight with a conversion problem looks nothing like what we would quote a company of two hundred with a
margin problem, and a number on this page would only be wrong for both. Every engagement is priced to the
problem and agreed in writing before any work begins.

## Questions people ask first

**FAQ:**

**Is the first conversation really free?**
Yes, and there is no follow-up sequence attached to it. You get an hour, our honest read, and no
obligation. If we both think there is something worth doing, we will tell you what that would look like.

**What if we are not sure what the problem is?**
That is the normal case, and it is the reason the first step is a conversation rather than a quote. Most
businesses can describe the symptom precisely and the cause not at all. Working out which is which is the
job.

**Do you only work with hospitality?**
No. The thinking was built and tested in a real trading business, our own venue, which is why the
examples on this site are specific rather than theoretical. The problems it solves are not
hospitality-shaped: demand, conversion, margin, operational drag, experience and scale look much the same
in a professional services firm or a trade business.

**Who will we actually be dealing with?**
Orange Jelly is deliberately small, so you are dealing with the people doing the work. There is no
account manager between you and the thinking.

**What happens to what we tell you?**
It is used to have one useful conversation and nothing else. No list, no sequence, no sharing it. The
answers you give on the second step of the form are commercially sensitive and are kept where only we can
read them. The [privacy notice](/privacy) has the detail.
