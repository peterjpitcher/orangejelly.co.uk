## How to read this

- **VERIFIED** = seen at the original source on 13 October 2026 (the BII's own site, magazine PDF, member email archive, annual report, or the publisher's own article).
- **SECONDARY** = a third party reporting it.
- **UNVERIFIED** = inference or pattern, not stated by a source.
- Quotes are exact and under 25 words. Every quoted string in this file was checked by script against the cached source text. Three quotes from one multi-column magazine page (Spring 2026 pp26 to 27) extract out of order, so they were confirmed by cropping the column.
- The BII's magazine uses spaced long dashes heavily. Quotes here were chosen to avoid that character, so some quotes stop short of a full sentence.

## Method and limits

- Sources read directly: bii.org (all sitemaps, 204 pages), seven BII News issues as PDF (Winter 2025 to Summer 2026), the BII's Mailchimp email archive, the BII annual report for 2025, ko-media.co.uk, biiab.co.uk, the Morning Advertiser sitemap (3,156 article URLs for 2025 and 2026), The Caterer sitemap (4,830 URLs dated 2025 and 2026), and Propel's own newsletter archive (464 daily briefings and 93 Friday Opinion editions, January 2025 to 13 October 2026).
- **Web search was not available.** The session's shared search budget was used up before this work started, so discovery relied on publishers' own sitemaps and archives. Consequences: Issuu and Yumpu were not checked (the BII hosts its own PDF editions, which are the primary digital edition), and trade press selection was made by URL slug and headline, so an article with an unhelpful slug could have been missed.
- BII News issues before Winter 2025 were not opened.
- INNfocus emails for August and early September 2026 are not in the bii.org archive (it stops at 27 July 2026) and the Mailchimp archive only lists the latest 20 campaigns (back to 9 September 2026), so that five-week gap was not read.

### 1.5 House style seen in comparable expert and partner columns

Page images saved for reference: `scratchpad/bii-data/pageimg/summer2026-52.png` (Orange Jelly page), `spring2026-26.jpg`, `spring2026-27.jpg` (AI feature), `spring2026-20.jpg` (Frazer Grant), `summer2026-65.jpg` (Trusted Partner page with QR code).

- **Section label first.** A small tag or a large section title ("Technology", "Events", "People & Training"), plus a "SPECIAL REPORT" stamp graphic inside a special report.
- **Headline.** Sentence case, bold sans serif, plain and literal. Often number-led or promise-led: "Five ways to turn summer footfall into summer revenue", "Your 90-day plan" with the second line "A GUIDE TO LIFTING YOUR BOTTOM LINE", "Great events can shine spotlight on your pub", "Seeing beyond the rota". Long explanatory headlines are accepted (the Spring 2026 AI headline runs to 18 words). No puns in the instructional pieces.
- **Standfirst.** 30 to 60 words, in bold colour or in capitals. It can sit above the headline (as on the Orange Jelly page). It is usually written by the editor in the third person and introduces the contributor: "Here, Peter Pitcher MBII suggests five things that he believes every pub should be doing, or doing better, this summer:" The same formula (Here, then the contributor's name, then explains) is used for the LTC and Proinsight.
- **Byline.** There is rarely a "By" line. The contributor is named in the standfirst with BII post-nominals (MBII, FBII, CBII) and their pub or company, with a round headshot.
- **Body.** Three columns, a drop capital, short paragraphs of two to four sentences, direct address ("you", "your pub").
- **Subheads.** Two to six words. In tips pieces they are numbered and imperative ("Create bookable reasons to visit", "Keep your Google listing fresh"). In features they are short capitals ("EVENTS THAT FILL THE PUB", "A PRACTICAL FIRST STEP").
- **Lists.** Numbered steps are preferred to bullets in the running text ("STEP 1", "WEEK ONE", or plain numerals one to five). Bullets appear in box-outs. Labels such as "Expert tip:", "TOP TIP:", "Goal:" and "The Result:" are common.
- **Pull quotes and box-outs.** One pull quote in capitals per page or spread, plus a tinted box with three or four takeaways.
- **Evidence.** Statistics are attributed in the sentence or in brackets to a named source, for example "(CGA, 2026)" or "KAM Insight data shows", with an asterisked footnote where needed. Named pubs and round pound figures are used freely.
- **Sign-off.** Editorial contributor pages end with a bio box and a footer strip: one line of boilerplate, "For more information visit:" and a URL, with a **QR code** at the right. Trusted Partner pages end with "TO GET IN TOUCH, SCAN THIS QR CODE", a phone number, an email address and any regulatory wording.
- **QR codes are routine.** They appear in the CEO's welcome, on campaign pages, on training pages ("please scan the QR code or go to www.bii.org/aplh") and on partner pages. The usual form is a QR code plus a short readable URL.
- **Tone.** Practical, peer to peer, warm and plain. First names after first mention. British spelling. The magazine celebrates operators and avoids lecturing.
- **Punctuation.** The magazine uses spaced long dashes throughout. Copy supplied without them may have them re-inserted in sub-editing.
- **AI disclosure precedent.** The Summer 2025 AI report said: "BII News has used AI, specifically ChatGPT, to help produce this feature, under the direction and editorship of Kate Oppenheim CBII". VERIFIED, Summer 2025 p47.
