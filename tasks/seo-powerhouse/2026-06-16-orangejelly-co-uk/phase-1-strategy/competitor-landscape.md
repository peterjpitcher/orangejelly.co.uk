## Key competitors

### 1. valueforvenues.co.uk — Greene King (content / AI-answer) — *the most strategically important*
- **Why relevant:** a brewery-resourced content hub ranking across **all four** of OJ's core informational clusters — "Social Media Marketing for Pubs: A Complete Guide", "12 Creative Event Ideas for Pubs (2026 Guide)", "How to Drive Pub Event Footfall". It is the direct content competitor for the very pages that carry OJ's traffic.
- **Where it wins:** deep, well-structured, freshly-dated guides; brand authority; breadth.
- **Where it's vulnerable:** it is brewery-brand content (tied perspective, promotes the Greene King ecosystem); generic, not from a single operator's lived results; no peer-to-peer voice.
- **What OJ learns / differentiates:** OJ's edge is the **real working publican** with **measured results at one venue** — concrete, dated, credible. Lead guides with "here's exactly what happened at our pub" plus approved CLAIMS. *(Note the repo constraint: Greene King = Tenant, not partner — keep that framing in any comparison copy.)*

### 2. CJ Digital (SERP / business — commercial)
- **Why relevant:** ranks for "digital marketing agency for pubs"; offers SEO, social, local ads — overlaps C1/C2 directly.
- **Vulnerable:** positions as a digital agency (the exact "agency" the time-poor, agency-sceptical licensee distrusts); no operator credibility.
- **Differentiation:** OJ is "a publican who'll do it for you", not an agency — lean into that against every agency competitor.

### 3. Wired Media (SERP / business + paid)
- **Why relevant:** full-service pub/bar agency with a dedicated PPC arm — competes organically and via paid on C1/C2.
- **Vulnerable:** broad hospitality generalist; price/retainer model implied; no single-venue proof.
- **Differentiation:** fixed packages from £375 + VAT and a 30-day action guarantee are concrete and low-risk vs an agency retainer.

### 4. Marketing For Pubs (SERP / business — commercial)
- **Why relevant:** exact-match brand for "marketing for pubs"; offers free audits + websites + social; decade of experience.
- **Vulnerable:** exact-match domain but thin operator proof; "free audit" funnel is generic.
- **Differentiation:** OJ's measured results + publican voice beat a generic free-audit hook.

### 5. smartpubtools.com (content / DIY-tool)
- **Why relevant:** ranks for "pub marketing ideas", "pub events that work", "pub social media ideas" — overlaps C4/C7 and represents the DIY alternative.
- **Vulnerable:** listicle/SEO-content style, tool-led, not experience-led.
- **Differentiation:** depth + real-venue specifics + a clear "or we'll do it for you" path the tools don't offer.

### 6. Morning Advertiser / CAMRA-style trade media (content)
- **Why relevant:** trade-news authority ranking for footfall/event topics (C4); high domain trust.
- **Vulnerable:** news angle, not how-to-with-proof; not a service provider.
- **Differentiation:** OJ offers actionable systems + done-for-you, not reportage.

## Strategic reading

- **Commercial cluster (C1–C3):** a crowded field of agencies. OJ's wedge is **anti-agency positioning** — a real publican, fixed transparent pricing, a guarantee, measured results. The job is not to out-agency the agencies; it is to be the credible non-agency choice and to make the service pages actually earn the clicks Google already gives OJ.
- **Informational clusters (C4–C8):** the threat is the **Greene King hub** out-resourcing OJ on the topics that feed its funnel. OJ cannot out-publish a brewery; it can **out-credible** it with first-hand, dated, results-backed guides in a peer voice — and convert those readers (which the brewery hub has no incentive to do for OJ).
- **AI-answer space:** content hubs (Greene King, smartpubtools) are structured for snippets/AI Overviews. OJ's FAQPage/Speakable schema + quotable answer blocks + a named expert author are the route to citation on C4–C7. The commercial cluster is not an AI-citation play.

## Differentiation opportunities (do what competitors aren't)

1. **Proof-led guides:** every core guide carries a real, dated outcome from The Anchor (approved CLAIMS only) — no agency or hub can match first-hand operator data.
2. **Anti-agency service pages:** explicitly "not an agency — a publican who does this for you", fixed packages, 30-day guarantee. Directly answers the audience's distrust.
3. **Info→service bridge:** competitors' content either doesn't sell (trade media, brewery hub) or sells generically (agencies). OJ can connect credible help to a low-risk service offer in one journey.
4. **Local + operator:** a regional location-page system fronted by a real operator is a combination the national agencies and the brewery hub don't offer.
