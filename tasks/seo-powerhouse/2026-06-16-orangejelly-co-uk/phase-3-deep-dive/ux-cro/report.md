# UX & Conversion Analysis — Orange Jelly (Phase 3)

**Date:** 2026-06-16 (Europe/London) · **Author:** UX/CRO Specialist
**Commercial goal:** more service enquiries / leads from UK licensees and pub operators.

**Data status:** GSC = **Known** (first-party). GA4 = **not supplied** -> no session/bounce/conversion/funnel-drop data exists; every behavioural claim below is `inferred` from code + GSC + manual page inspection, never fabricated. No PSI/CrUX -> no Core Web Vitals field data. Live-page checks via `ctx_fetch_and_index` (manual inspection). Code locations are `file:line` (Known).

## Summary

The site's biggest commercial blocker is **not traffic — it is the conversion layer, and it fails in three compounding ways**:

1. **The one place that captures an enquiry by email — the contact form — does not deliver the enquiry anywhere.** The server action only `console.log`s the submission (`src/app/actions/contact.ts:30`); there is no email, CRM, or database write. Any licensee who fills the form sees "Message sent successfully" while the lead is silently lost. This is the single most damaging defect on the site and it invalidates the whole enquiry funnel. (Severity: Critical.)

2. **Almost every conversion path funnels to WhatsApp, not to a captured lead.** The Hero CTA, service pages, the `/services` hub, the guide template, the nav (desktop + mobile), and two of three sticky bars all push `wa.me`. WhatsApp is high-friction for a sceptical, time-poor licensee who has just met the brand (it demands they open a messaging app and start a conversation with a stranger using their personal number), it leaves **no contact record if they don't send**, and — critically — **WhatsApp clicks are completely untracked** (Analytics SEO-001), so the business cannot see whether the dominant CTA works. The strongest commercial pages (`/pub-marketing-agency`, homepage `PackageCTA`) already offer a balanced "WhatsApp **or** enquiry form" choice with the 30-day guarantee — that pattern exists and converts intent-to-email; it simply is not applied to the pages that rank for buying intent.

3. **The pages Google ranks for buying intent are either un-indexed, redirected, or send the visitor to a WhatsApp-only template with no form.** GSC shows `instagram services for pubs` at pos 7.0 (256 impr, 0 clicks) and `facebook services for pubs` at pos 6.1 (123 impr, 0 clicks) — but `/services/instagram-services-for-pubs` and `/services/facebook-services-for-pubs` are **308 redirects** (`page.tsx:4 permanentRedirect`) to `/services/social-media-marketing-for-pubs`, and that destination uses the `PubServiceLandingPage` template, which has **no enquiry form and no `/contact` link at all** — WhatsApp is the only conversion action (`PubServiceLandingPage.tsx:204`). So even the buyers Google already delivers near the top of page one land on a high-friction, single-channel page. The wider commercial set (`/services` itself, the `/pub-marketing-*` regions, `/ways-to-work/*`) is in the not-indexed bucket per Analytics/Technical Phase 2 — meaning the funnel is capped at ~0 before UX even matters.

**The order of fixes is therefore: (a) make the contact form actually deliver leads + track CTA clicks [Analytics SEO-001], (b) put a balanced WhatsApp-or-form enquiry block on every commercial + guide template, (c) let Technical un-block indexation [SEO-004].** UX cannot manufacture demand — GSC proves the demand is already there. The job is to stop leaking it.

A secondary, sitewide problem is **destination chaos**: `/services`, `/ways-to-work`, `/capabilities`, `/pub-marketing`, `/pub-marketing-agency` are five competing "what we do / hire us" hubs with overlapping content and inconsistent CTAs. The navigation surfaces "Ways to Work" and "Capabilities" (insider jargon) but **never surfaces `/services`** (the word buyers actually search and the page Google indexes for the commercial cluster). A licensee arriving on a guide has no clear, single, repeatable next step.

## User Journey Gaps

- **Dead-end on guides:** the guide end-CTA sends to `/ways-to-work` and `/capabilities` — neither matches the article topic. A reader of "social media strategy for pubs" should see a one-click bridge to the social-media service, not a generic packages page. Currently only **27 of 106 guides** reference any service/commercial link at all (`grep` over `content/blog/`).
- **`/services` is orphaned from the primary nav.** Buyers search "services"/"marketing for pubs"; the menu offers "Ways to Work" + "Capabilities". `/contact` is linked from only ~8 places sitewide.
- **Five competing commercial hubs** (`/services`, `/ways-to-work`, `/capabilities`, `/pub-marketing`, `/pub-marketing-agency`) dilute internal-link equity and confuse the next step. There is no single canonical "hire us" destination.
- **No cross-link between the ranking service pages and the matching guides** (or vice versa), so a licensee who proves a guide useful is never offered the paid version of that exact thing.
- **WhatsApp-as-only-CTA assumes the visitor is ready to message a stranger now.** For a sceptical, time-poor audience mid-research, the lower-commitment "send an enquiry" form is the bridge — but it's hidden on most pages and broken where present.

## Mobile experience (commercially material — mobile out-ranks/out-converts desktop per GSC)

GSC Devices (Known): Mobile pos 9.83 / CTR 1.87% vs Desktop 13.61 / 0.92% — **mobile is the surface that earns the clicks**, so mobile conversion is the priority.

- **Three competing bottom-anchored interrupts on mobile guides** (`StickyEngagementBar` + blog `StickyCTA` + `MobileScrollPrompt`) create visual clutter and can obscure content/CTAs. Consolidate to one. (`inferred` from code; verify on a real device at 375px.)
- WhatsApp buttons meet the 44px tap-target minimum (`WhatsAppButton.tsx:52-55`) — good.
- The contact form is single-column and uses 44px min submit (`contact-form.tsx:219`) — structurally fine on mobile; the defect is delivery, not layout.
- `maximumScale: 5, userScalable: true` (`layout.tsx:34`) — accessibility-correct, keep.

## Page Speed & Visual Stability (UX perspective; no CrUX field data)

- The `/services/*` redirect -> `loading.tsx` shell ("Loading Orange Jelly...") flashes before the destination renders. On the buyer-intent URLs this is both a crawl signal and a perceived-slowness/instability hit at the worst moment. Confirm the redirect resolves server-side (308) without a client loading flash. (`inferred`; Technical owns the redirect/render mechanism.)
- Hero uses a full-bleed `priority` background image with heavy filters (`Hero.tsx:49-57`); combined with the 3 oversized images flagged in the crawl, this risks LCP/CLS on mobile. Defer to Technical for field metrics; UX recommendation is to ensure hero image is sized/compressed and the H1 text paints first.

## Quick UX Wins (high impact, low effort — ship first)

1. **Wire the contact form to actually deliver leads** (`contact.ts`) — recovers every form enquiry. Pair with `enquiry_submit` tracking (SEO-001). *Critical, Small-Medium.*
2. **Add the dual WhatsApp-or-enquiry-form block (the `PackageCTA` pattern) to the `PubServiceLandingPage` template** — gives every service page an email-capture path, not just WhatsApp. One template edit covers all service pages. *High, Small.*
3. **Track every WhatsApp / `tel:` / `mailto:` click** (SEO-001) so the dominant CTA is finally measurable. *High, Small — Analytics owns.*
4. **Add `/services` (or a clearly-labelled "Services / What we do for pubs") to the primary nav** and pick ONE canonical commercial hub; reduce the five competing hubs. *Medium, Small-Medium.*
5. **Replace the generic guide end-CTA with an intent-matched service bridge** (template-level: map category -> service page) offering WhatsApp + form. *High, Medium — Content/UX/dev.*
6. **De-duplicate the sticky/interrupt stack on mobile guides** to a single CTA. *Medium, Small.*

Fixing them does not require any new ranking. Page-by-page tickets are in `landing-page-recommendations.md`.
