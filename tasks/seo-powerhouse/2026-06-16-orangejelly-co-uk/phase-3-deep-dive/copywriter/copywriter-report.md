## Summary

I assessed the priority commercial pages and the top informational guides against the Phase 1 strategy and the Phase 2 technical/content/analytics findings, verifying current metadata in code (`src/lib/seo-overrides.ts` — 101 parsed entries — and each route's `generateMetadata`) and current on-page structure in the source.

**The single most important finding for this role:** the brief's premise that the top guides need title/meta rewrites is **largely already solved**. The guide metadata was recently rebuilt and is genuinely good — titles 46–55 chars, keyword-first, descriptions 130–153 chars carrying approved CLAIMS percentages. Rewriting them would be change-for-change's-sake and risks regressing a working setup. **I therefore re-pointed the copywriting effort at the two places where copy actually moves enquiries:**

1. **The commercial-page conversion failure (the ~0.07% CTR on 2,908 impressions).** This is NOT a metadata problem and NOT primarily a ranking problem — Google already shows these pages at pos 6–20. It is a **page-body + positioning** problem: the pages don't earn the click in the SERP (no differentiation against agency competitors) and don't earn the enquiry on the page (weak proof, buried price, generic CTAs). The fix is body copy: quotable opener answer blocks, the full five-CLAIM proof, transparent packages-from pricing, the 30-day guarantee, and a single primary CTA to `/contact`. **Biggest single lever:** `/services/social-media-marketing-for-pubs` inherits pos-6/7 rankings from the Facebook/Instagram redirect stubs but never names those channels prominently on-page — adding "Facebook for your pub" and "Instagram for your pub" H2 sections is the highest CTR-recovery move on the site.

2. **The info→commercial bridge.** Verified in code: the guide bridge is `getCategoryCTA()` in `BlogPost.tsx` — a generic, 4-variant, category-based block whose button is always "See Our Packages → /ways-to-work". `internal-links.csv` confirms ~38 evenly-distributed guide→commercial links (a sitewide block, not topic-matched). The fix is a **template change** to make the bridge cluster-specific with one matching service link and peer-to-peer voice (full mapping in `page-recommendations.md` §3). This converts the ~458 informational clicks/12mo the site already earns — no new ranking required.

**Patterns (so the orchestrator can collapse to system fixes):**
- **Meta descriptions over 160 chars** on several commercial pages (`/pub-marketing-agency` 165, `/services/social-media...` 172, `/services/paid-social` 174, `/services/content-creation` 165, `/fix-my-pub` 181) — all truncate in the SERP. This is a repeating issue → **one Content-process fix** (a ≤160-char description discipline + a build-time length lint), not five one-offs. Positioning in each is already correct; only length is wrong.
- **Two titles over 60 chars:** `/ways-to-work` (65) and homepage (61). Minor; trim.
- **Commercial pages not indexed** (Technical C-3/C-4) gate every commercial copy change — copy converts nothing until the page is in the index. Dependency noted on every commercial ticket.
- **Dual-H1 defect** (Technical C-1) dilutes the topic signal on the exact guides this role wants to position-improve — the copywriting position plays depend on that template fix landing.

**Quality level:** metadata foundation strong; commercial body copy thin-for-SERP and under-converting; guides deep and well-titled but routing readers to a generic hub. No invented metrics introduced; every proof point maps to `/CLAIMS.md`.

---

## What I did NOT recommend (and why)

- **No guide title/meta rewrites** where the current copy is already good — over-optimisation hurts rankings and conversions (role rule). I marked each "keep".
- **No live indexation/redirect/schema/canonical change** — all routed to the Phase 5 Risk Register (e.g. the Facebook/Instagram un-redirect decision, the rescue-page consolidation).
- **No new commercial-term volume claims** — the family-events page and any FB/IG un-redirect decision are gated on keyword-plan / GKP validation.
- **No new pages originated** — Content Strategist owns origination; I provided the conversion-copy spec for the one validated gap.

---

## Tie-back to commercial goal

The lead-gen goal is served in priority order: (1) **commercial-page body copy** captures the 2,908 shown impressions currently earning 2 clicks — directly the enquiry lever; (2) the **topic-matched bridge** converts existing informational authority into enquiries with no new ranking; (3) **position-improvement copy** on top guides (paired with the dual-H1 fix) lifts pos-12–16 pages into the click range. None is provable until GA4 enquiry tracking (SEO-001) and commercial-page indexation (Technical C-3/C-4) land — both are upstream dependencies, not copywriter deliverables.
