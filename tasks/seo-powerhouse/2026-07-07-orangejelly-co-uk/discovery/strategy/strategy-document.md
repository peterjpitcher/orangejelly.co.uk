## 1. Business context

Orange Jelly is a UK pub consultancy run by Peter Pitcher (The Anchor, Stanwell Moor TW19 6AQ). £75/hour plus VAT, packages from £375 plus VAT, 30-day guarantee. Audience: struggling UK pub licensees. Commercial success = **service enquiries**, not rankings. Approved claim metrics live only in `/CLAIMS.md` (percentages only).

## 4. Where Orange Jelly can realistically win (next 6 months)

Honest constraint first: with ~25 clicks/28 days and no backlink-tool baseline, Orange Jelly cannot win head terms against established agencies or Greene King's content hub. It wins where it already holds positions or unambiguous topical authority:

1. **Named-channel service queries it already ranks for** ("instagram services for pubs" pos 7.0, "facebook services for pubs" pos 6.1, "social media marketing for pubs" pos 12, "paid social for pubs" pos 11.2, "content creation for pubs" pos 14.8; and in 28-day data "content creation services for pubs" **pos 2.0**). These are inherited page-one/near-page-one rankings pointing at thin or self-cancelling pages. Fixing the pages recovers clicks with **zero new rankings required**. Est. from `evidence/opportunities-ctr-gap.csv`: ~26 clicks/yr left on the table across the five channel queries alone — small absolutely, but these are the highest-intent visitors the site can get.
2. **Rescue/turnaround** ("fix my pub" pos 5.3–5.7, "empty pub" pos 8.4). SERP research (see `competitor-landscape.md`) shows the "pub rescue" SERP is dominated by insolvency firms — closure intent, not recovery intent. Orange Jelly's recovery-first framing is genuinely differentiated on this SERP and it already ranks. CTR recovery + indexation of the supporting pages.
3. **The events/quiz informational engine** (6,100+ impr across two clusters) — already winning; the job is (a) position-improvement passes on high-impression pos 10–20 guides, (b) proving the bridge converts via measurement, (c) AEO answer-blocks so the guides earn AI-answer citations (smartpubtools.com and Greene King's valueforvenues.co.uk are actively competing here with 2026-dated content).
4. **"Pub marketing (agency)"** (666 + 304 + 161 impr, pos 18–25, 0 clicks) — winnable only after the hub cannibalisation is resolved (five competing "hire us" hubs; June CAN-5/SEO-029) and the winning hub is indexed. Medium-term.

**Where it cannot win / should stop:** county-page expansion (total local demand 12mo = 30 impressions across 9 queries — keep the 9 pages, build nothing more); FAQ rich-result chasing (retired); broad "digital marketing for pubs" head-term content against agencies with case-study depth; any net-new content pillar without `keyword-plan` GKP validation (the family/kids events pillar remains the one validated candidate, still **blocked on keyword-plan validation**).
