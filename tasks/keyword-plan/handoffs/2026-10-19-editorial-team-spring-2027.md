## Rules that bind every brief below

- **The keyword lists here are the source of truth.** Do not substitute your own.
- **What "band" means.** Every band below is a Keyword Planner monthly search range for one
  keyword: United Kingdom, English, Google Search, window 1 October 2025 to 30 September 2026,
  precision `encoded_band`, observed 2026-10-17, from `staged/demand-observations.csv`. A band is a
  range, never a count. Never print a single number from it and never add bands together.
- **No performance figures exist.** This run had no Search Console pulls, so there are no clicks,
  impressions or positions for any of these groups. Nothing below is a measure of how the site does.
- **No scenario is estimable.** Every group is evidence tier C (demand only) and the playbook is
  tier D (no measurable demand). Nobody may promise a click or traffic number from this work.
- **Date terms are answered in passing, never targeted.** People searching a date are mostly not
  licensees. Give the date plainly in the page; do not build the title or headings around it.
- **No answer blocks on assumption.** No result page has been observed for any of these terms, so
  do not shape FAQ or answer blocks around what the results page might look like.
- **House rules.** British English, no em dashes, only the five approved claims in `/CLAIMS.md`
  (percentages, attributed to The Anchor), no invented facts, every date checked against an official
  source, The Anchor described as our own venue. The pre-commit guards reject cost-reduction
  phrasing and American spellings; write around them.
- **Avoid list.** Read the spring 2027 section of `config.toml` before choosing any phrase: "near
  me", past years, home cooking and crafts, and children's and school terms are ruled out.

- **Pub food group.** The occasion menu terms that the pub food and menu profitability group

  `staged/overlaps.csv`. The new guides own their occasion menus; that group's page,

- **Events calendar.** "Pub events calendar" belongs to `/guides/seasonal-pub-events-calendar`. The
  hub links to it rather than competing.
- **Existing mentions.** `seasonal-pub-events-calendar` and `pub-marketing-plan-2026-monthly-guide`
  each give these occasions a paragraph. No existing guide is dedicated to any of the five, so
  there is no page to merge; the new guides go deeper and the older pages should link to them.
- **Site events.** `site-events.toml` does not exist yet. Nothing in this work compares pages
  across the `/licensees-guide/` to `/guides/` rename.
